## 1. Design Token Consumption in Email Templates

Transactional HTML emails are notorious for aesthetic drift. Because external mail clients (such as Microsoft Outlook desktop, Gmail web, and Apple Mail) strip `<link>` tags and external stylesheet references, design tokens cannot be loaded via runtime CSS variables (`var(--color-primary)`).

To guarantee strict brand fidelity, our email generation worker ([`lib/security/email-worker.ts`](file:///c:/Users/DT001/Desktop/SchoolFix/lib/security/email-worker.ts)) compiles tokens into embedded, client-compatible inline styles:
- **Header Background:** `#4B0082` (Pinned Primary 40 Indigo from Task 1 tokens).
- **Text On-Header:** `#FFFFFF` (10.2:1 contrast ratio, AAA compliant).
- **Surface Body:** `#FFFFFF` on `#F5F5F7` canvas.
- **Card Border & Shadow:** Brand-tinted Indigo border (`rgba(75, 0, 130, 0.12)`) and soft elevation shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(75, 0, 130, 0.08)`).
- **OTP Box:** Soft lavender tint (`#F3E8FF`) with a 2px dashed brand border (`#4B0082`), preventing generic gray appearance.

## 2. Critique of Agent-Generated Copy

The initial copy generated by the AI agent read:
> *"Dear Zainab Balogun, Welcome to SchoolFix. To complete your enrollment and access tuition reconciliation and academic records for your dependent pupils, please enter the following 6-digit confirmation code: 241925. Valid for 10 minutes. If you did not initiate this enrollment request, please notify the school bursary immediately."*

### What Is Technically Competent About It
- It is functional and unambiguous.
- It clearly specifies the purpose (access to tuition reconciliation and academic records).
- It clearly names the time constraint (10 minutes) and the failure remediation channel (bursary notification).

### What A Professional Developer or Marketer Would Change for Real Customers

In a real Nigerian private school deployment, this copy would be rejected by school proprietors and replaced for three operational reasons:

1. **Missing School Co-Branding:**
   - *Problem:* Nigerian parents do not enroll in "SchoolFix Technologies"; they enroll their children in *"Corona Secondary School, Gbagada"* or *"Kingsway Academy, Kano"*. An email stating solely *"Welcome to SchoolFix"* causes parents to suspect phishing, spam, or an unauthorized loan app.
   - *Fix:* The copy must dynamically feature the subscribing school's name in the header and body:  
     > *"Welcome to the official Parent Portal for **Corona Secondary School** (Powered by SchoolFix)."*

2. **Overly Bureaucratic Tone ("Tuition Reconciliation" & "Dependent Pupils"):**
   - *Problem:* Terms like *"tuition reconciliation"* and *"dependent pupils"* sound like ERP database jargon. Real Nigerian parents talk about *"paying school fees"*, *"downloading term report cards"*, and *"checking child attendance"*.
   - *Fix:* Rewrite the value proposition into plain, empathetic parent language:  
     > *"You are one step away from viewing your child's First Term grades and generating official fee clearance receipts."*

3. **Missing Immediate Fraud Contact Mechanism:**
   - *Problem:* The copy advises parents to *"notify the school bursary immediately"* if they did not initiate the request, but provides no phone number, WhatsApp link, or support email! A panicked parent who suspects identity theft has no way to act on the warning.
   - *Fix:* Include the school bursary's direct WhatsApp reconciliation line:  
     > *"Did not request this? Report immediately to the Bursary Helpdesk via WhatsApp: +234 803 000 1122."*
