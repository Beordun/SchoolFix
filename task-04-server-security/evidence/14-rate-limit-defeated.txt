--- ARCHITECTURAL LESSON & LIMITS OF IP RATE LIMITING ---
1. Why IP Rate Limiting Alone is Insufficient:
   In distributed mobile environments, IP addresses are volatile. In Nigeria, mobile telcos (MTN, Airtel, Glo) employ Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT) where thousands of legitimate mobile subscribers share a tiny pool of public IPv4 addresses. 
   - An attacker on a botnet or rotating residential proxy network easily defeats IP rate limiting by cycling IP addresses.
   - Conversely, locking out a single public IP can inadvertently lock out an entire school staff room or university campus where 50 teachers browse through one shared school Wi-Fi router.
2. The Multi-Key Defense:
   Production security must key rate limiters on a combination of:
   - Account identifier (Normalized Email: prevents brute-forcing a specific parent account from distributed IPs).
   - Device fingerprints / TLS Client Hello JA4 fingerprints.
   - Global endpoint budgets (protecting overall server capacity against DDoS).
