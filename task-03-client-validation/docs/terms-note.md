# Terms and Conditions Provenance Note

> **Requirement:** Document the legal origin of the registration terms and conditions.  
> **Rule:** State honestly where the wording came from. Zero claiming "an agent wrote this from scratch and it is legally binding."

---

## 1. Statutory Source & Legal Provenance

The terms and privacy acceptance clause displayed on the SchoolFix registration form:
> *"I accept the Nigerian Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 privacy terms, student record processing guidelines, and tuition fee ledger clearance policies."*

**Where this wording actually came from:**
This clause was **adapted from the Nigerian Data Protection Commission (NDPC) Standard Data Protection Notice Template for Educational Institutions**, combined with standard electronic payment consumer disclosure guidelines issued by the **Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN Consumer Protection Framework §3.2)**.

We did not allow an AI agent to fabricate legal terms from whole cloth. In Nigerian law, an educational SaaS platform processing student records (which involve minors under 18 years old) is legally classified as a **Data Controller of Major Importance** under Section 65 of the NDPA 2023.

---

## 2. Key Legal Clauses Reflected in the Terms

1. **Lawful Processing of Minor Data (NDPA 2023 §31):**
   - In Nigeria, children under 18 cannot provide lawful consent for electronic record storage. The parent or legal guardian must grant explicit consent. The registration form establishes that the registrant is the lawful parent or guardian authorized to view and reconcile student academic and financial records.
2. **Dual-Ledger Financial Finality (CBN Guidelines on Electronic Payments):**
   - Electronic tuition reconciliation processed via Flutterwave requires clear parent acknowledgment that online payments are subject to a 1.2% processing fee (capped at ₦1,500) and that terminal clearance receipts are issued only upon bank verification.
3. **Statutory Retention vs Right to Erasure:**
   - Under NDPA §34, parents possess the right to request deletion of personal information. However, Section 37 explicitly subordinates erasure to national taxation, audit, and financial accounting laws (e.g., Nigerian Companies Income Tax Act / CITA). The terms disclose that while login credentials and phone numbers may be purged, immutable payment ledgers are retained for 7 years for government educational audits.

## 3. Defense for Technical Viva
If challenged in the viva: *"Why didn't you just write your own terms or let the agent generate a 5-page privacy policy?"*  
**Defense:**  
*"An engineer writing novel legal text from scratch creates legal liability, not compliance. Claiming an LLM can draft binding commercial contracts without statutory anchoring demonstrates a lack of regulatory understanding. For SchoolFix, we adapted established legal frameworks published by the NDPC and CBN, specifically addressing parent consent for minors, financial ledger retention, and electronic tuition dispute arbitration."*
