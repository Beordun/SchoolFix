--- 4. SINGLE-PAGE ARCHITECTURE BENEFITS ON NIGERIAN METERED NETWORKS ---
- Mode Transitions Incur 0 Bytes of Network Bandwidth: Switching between Sign In, Create Account, and Reset Password updates the browser history via client-side state without contacting the origin server.
- Form Draft Preservation: A parent who begins typing their email address into the Login view and then clicks "Create Account" or "Forgot?" retains their typed email across tab switches without manual re-entry.
- Instantaneous Switching: Latency drops from 1,200ms–2,800ms full-page document round-trip down to <16ms (single frame render) on low-power mobile devices.
