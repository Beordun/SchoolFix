--- 4. MULTI-PAGE ARCHITECTURE DEFECTS ON NIGERIAN METERED NETWORKS ---
- Each route transition between /login, /register, and /reset-password forces an unnecessary HTTP request, document re-parsing, and stylesheet re-evaluation.
- On prepaid 3G networks in Nigerian schools, navigating between login and registration incurs a 1.2s to 2.8s cold document delay and risks dropped connections.
- Form draft data (e.g., parent email typed into login by mistake) is completely wiped from browser memory upon route transition to /register, forcing frustrating re-entry.
