# SchoolFix: Business Brief

### 1. The Business
SchoolFix is an administrative operating platform for private nursery, primary, and secondary schools in peri-urban and urban Nigeria. These schools run on tight termly cash reserves, depend heavily on timely tuition collection, and manage between 150 and 600 pupils per campus. SchoolFix digitises student academic tracking, daily registers, and fee settlement reconciliation into one low-friction system.

### 2. The User Persona
The primary daily user is Mrs. Folashade Adeleke, a 34-year-old classroom teacher and level coordinator. 

* **Demographics:** Age 26 to 48. Non-technical; comfortable with WhatsApp and mobile banking, but alienated by complex enterprise software.
* **Device:** Mid-tier Android smartphone (predominantly 3GB RAM, running Android 11 to 13) bought secondhand or on credit. Secondary access on a shared desktop in the administrative office.
* **Network Reality:** Intermittent 3G and 4G connections with variable bandwidth, high packet drops, and metered personal data bundles.

### 3. The One Thing That Must Work
Remote student record and payment ledger lookups on low-bandwidth mobile connections. 

Teachers and administrators must be able to verify fee clearance and update student academic records immediately without session timeouts, bloated asset loads, or data loss caused by network drops.

### 4. Three Questions Only This Business Can Answer
1. Has this pupil completed their second-term tuition and development levy, or do they have an outstanding arrears balance preventing exam clearance?
2. What is the verified termly attendance and continuous assessment score for this pupil across subjects before compiling end-of-term report sheets?
3. Which parents have unread administrative notices regarding school bus logistics, upcoming PTA meetings, or fee payment deadlines?

### 5. Interface Character and Typography Rationale
The interface must feel authoritative, utilitarian, and lightweight rather than decorative. 

Private schools handle disputes over fees and grades where tension between parents and school owners runs high. The UI must convey clarity, calm, and institutional trust, avoiding flashy animations that exhaust low-end device processors or consume prepaid data bundles.

* **Typography Direction:** A clean, highly legible grotesque or geometric sans-serif typeface with open counters and generous x-height (such as Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter). 
* **Justification:** High scannability is essential for tired teachers keying in attendance and marks on cracked 5.5-inch screens under harsh classroom lighting, ensuring distinct numerical readability so figures like 3, 8, 0, and 6 are never misread.
